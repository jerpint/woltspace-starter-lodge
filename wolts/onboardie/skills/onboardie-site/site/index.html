What is woltspace?

Woltspace is a lodge for wolts: creatures that live on your own machine and work with you. Each wolt has its own name, its own personality, its own memory and its own little website. You talk to them, they build and think with you, and they remember what you did together.

Everything stays in your lodge, and everything is private unless you decide to share it.

How it works

The lodge

Your home page. Every wolt lives here. Open one to talk to it and see its site side by side.

Wolts

Each wolt is a folder: who it is, what it remembers, what it made. Give each one a clear job.

Creatures

A wolt's creature is its temperament: raccoons think, beavers build, otters are quick.

Sessions

Each conversation is a session. Idle ones rest to save resources, and pick up where they left off when you come back.

Memory

Wolts write down what matters in plain files they read at the start of every session. You can read them too.

Sites

Every rodent wolt has a site, like this one. It's their desk and their notebook - private by default.

Apps

When something needs its own server, it becomes an app: its own address, still private until you share it.

Sharing

Nothing is shared by accident. You share one thing at a time, with the people you choose.

Your first wolt

  1. Pick one job. "Helps me write my newsletter." "Keeps my side project moving." One wolt, one job works best.
  2. Pick a creature. A raccoon if the job is mostly thinking and deciding, a beaver if it's mostly building, an otter if it's lots of small quick things.
  3. Pick a name you like. Lowercase, short, yours.
  4. Create it from the lodge and say hi. Its first session introduces itself and makes its site its own.

Not sure what to pick? Tell me what you came here to do, and we'll figure it out together. I can build a little too - but your own wolts will know your work and grow with you. That's the real fun.
